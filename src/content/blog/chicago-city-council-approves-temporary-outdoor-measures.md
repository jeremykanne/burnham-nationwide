On Tuesday, December 17, Chicago City Council passed legislation in support of extended regulatory relief for businesses. This comes at a time when Chicago businesses have to adapt to the phase three reopening. 

**Sidewalk Café Reforms**  
All current Sidewalk Café Permits will be **considered active through May 31, 2021** and businesses with a Sidewalk Café Permit can operate their current Sidewalk Cafes until that date without renewing. There will be temporary adjustments to the sidewalk café permit to reduce costs, expand the opportunity for revenue and expedite the process to obtain a permit.

-   Permit fees issued for the 2021 season will benefit from temporary reforms implemented earlier this year: permit fee reduction of 75 percent
-   They will be able to expand front of a neighbor's frontage, which was previously prohibited.
    -   A new application for this extension will be required and it's essential to receive local Alderman approval. 

-   The permitting process has been expedited for timely use.

Is the 75% sidewalk café permit fee reduction mentioned in this post still available? No - that was a temporary COVID-era relief measure specific to the 2021 permit season and has long since expired. Current sidewalk café permit fees follow the city's standard fee schedule rather than any pandemic-era discount. Can a sidewalk café still expand in front of a neighboring business's frontage today? Not under this specific temporary provision - that expansion option was tied to the 2021 COVID relief measures. Under standing rules, a sidewalk café is generally limited to the frontage of the business operating it, absent a specific separate agreement or approval process. Does getting Alderman approval still matter for sidewalk café permits today? Yes - this is one piece of the post that reflects a lasting process feature rather than a temporary one. Alderman sign-off remains a standard part of getting sidewalk café permit applications (and certain permit modifications) approved in Chicago, independent of any pandemic-era reform. Were sidewalk café permits ever allowed to stay active without renewal again after this 2021 extension? No - that grace period (permits considered active through May 31, 2021 without renewal) was a one-time pandemic accommodation. Current sidewalk café permits follow the standard annual renewal cycle with no automatic extension.
