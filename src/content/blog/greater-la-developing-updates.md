Burnham's goal, as always, is to continue communicating with city officials online, via email and by phone and provide you with updates on any city implementation that may take place in the coming weeks. We will continue to monitor all jurisdictions for updates and will send out new processes as they’re announced. 

**Updates as of March 23, 2020**

Los Angeles

-   All Development Service Centers will be closed to the public. LADBS and other departments are encouraging the use of their online portals and communication via phone or video conference  
    
-   The Downtown Fig Plaza, Van Nuys Marvin Braude, and West LA offices will all have customer "Pick-Up/Drop-Off" centers in the lobby  
    -   All other departments will also utilize the same "Pick-Up/Drop-off" system as B&S  
        
-   Over-the-Counter reviews have transitioned to 1-2 business day backroom reviews  
    
-   Re-submissions will be via drop-off and scheduled video conference  
    
-   All essential field operations (inspections) will continue as normal. Please communicate as much as possible through phone or email with your field inspectors.  
    

Greater Los Angeles/Orange County/San Diego area  

-   Many have closed their City Halls to the public and have adopted a form of online submittals via email or online portal or have a similar drop-off method.
-   Fore more information regarding additional jurisdiction updates contact your local Burnham Project Manager.

Please contact your Burnham Project Manager or email us at LA@burnhamnationwide.com for any specific questions or should you need any additional information on how this impacts your current or future projects in any California jurisdiction.

**Updates of major jurisdictions - March 20**

As of late night, Los Angeles County officials announced a “Safer at Home” policy. Governor Newsom followed up with an announcement of a similar order minutes later.

Are LADBS Development Service Centers still closed to the public, as described in this post? No, this was a temporary March 2020 pandemic measure. LADBS Development Services Centers (Metro, Van Nuys, West LA, South LA, and San Pedro) have long since reopened for in-person services, alongside the digital ePlanLA and PermitLA systems that have become the city's standard submittal channels. Is San Diego's Development Services still limiting visitors and quarantining plans for 24 hours before screening, as this post describes? No, these were specific, temporary precautions tied to the earliest phase of the pandemic response and aren't current operating procedures. San Diego's Development Services Department now operates under standard, non-pandemic procedures.  
Are inspections still suspended in San Diego, as mentioned in this post? No, that suspension was specific to the initial March 2020 shutdown period. Inspections (including remote/virtual options, as covered elsewhere in this blog) have resumed and operate under standard scheduling citywide.  
Is Los Angeles County's building department still closed, requiring email-only case-by-case resubmittals as described here? No, this reflects the very earliest, most restrictive phase of LA County's 2020 shutdown response and has no bearing on current operations, which have returned to standard in-person and electronic submittal processes. Where can I find current operating status for LA, Orange County, or San Diego building departments instead of this 2020 snapshot? Each jurisdiction's building department website is the best current source, LADBS, San Diego Development Services, and Orange County agencies have all returned to standard operations with their own current digital and in-person submittal systems, none of which resemble the emergency drop-off/dropbox procedures described in this 2020 post.
