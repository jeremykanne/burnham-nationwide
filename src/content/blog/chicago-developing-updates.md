Can hospitals and healthcare facilities still bypass Chicago DOB permits for temporary structures, as described in this post? No, this was a specific, time-limited accommodation (effective for 90 calendar days starting March 23, 2020) tied to the early COVID-19 healthcare capacity emergency, and it expired by its own terms years ago. Temporary structures for healthcare functions today require standard DOB permitting. Is Illinois' Stay-At-Home order from March 2020 still in effect? No, Governor Pritzker's order had a defined initial end date (April 7, 2020) and the broader state of emergency framework around it has long since concluded. There's no active stay-at-home order or essential/non-essential business distinction in Illinois today. Is Chicago City Hall still closed to the public, as mentioned in this post? No, this was a temporary closure specific to the earliest phase of Chicago's pandemic response. City Hall and DOB offices have long since returned to standard public access alongside the online submission systems that were expanded during this period. Is the "Interim Procedure for Online Submission of Easy Permits" referenced in this post still the current process? That specific interim, pandemic-era workaround has been superseded, Chicago's permit review tracks (covered in more depth in this blog's "Three Different Types of Chicago Plan Reviews" post) reflect the city's current, standard process structure rather than this temporary 2020 accommodation. Is Lori Lightfoot still the Mayor of Chicago, as referenced in this post? No, as covered in an earlier post in this batch, Lightfoot's term ended in 2023, and Brandon Johnson is the current mayor.
