**New York City Updates April 22**

All permits issued by the Department of Buildings that are expiring between March 12, 2020 through at least May 9, 2020 **will be extended** in accordance with [Mayoral Emergency Executive Order No. 107](https://www1.nyc.gov/assets/home/downloads/pdf/executive-orders/2020/eeo-107.pdf?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-9Rfja9GOVDxTxbZbdDG4LQQus3XQ1LMdQkfcEEBTpFvXmay11u9yLqQ7JJacVLIE5JnAdH). 

-   **Permit Renewal Extension**   
    Permits already in effect will remain in effect until the end of the term for which they were issued. Permits that *would have* *expired* during the state of emergency have been extended for the duration of the state of emergency. In the case that the state of emergency is extended, the permit expiration date will automatically be extended.  
    
-   **Insurance Requirements**  
    While the expiration date of the permits has been extended, [valid insurance required](http://a810-bisweb.nyc.gov/bisweb/bsqpm01.jsp?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-9Rfja9GOVDxTxbZbdDG4LQQus3XQ1LMdQkfcEEBTpFvXmay11u9yLqQ7JJacVLIE5JnAdH) by law or rule is still required and no work can be performed without updated insurance under these extended permits.  
    
-   **License Renewal Extension**  
    The Mayoral Emergency Executive Order also applies to all [licenses/registrations](https://www1.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/pdf/covid-19_license_renewal_extension_sn.pdf?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-9Rfja9GOVDxTxbZbdDG4LQQus3XQ1LMdQkfcEEBTpFvXmay11u9yLqQ7JJacVLIE5JnAdH) issued by the Department of Buildings. The Department is in the process of transitioning to a system of online license renewals. As we receive updates, we will share them with you as well.

Are non-essential construction sites still suspended in NYC, as this post describes? No, this was a specific, temporary measure tied to the initial COVID-19 state of emergency in March 2020 and has no bearing on current construction activity. NYC construction operates under normal permitting rules today, with no essential/non-essential distinction in effect. Do DOB transactions still need to be mailed or hand-delivered to borough offices, as outlined in this post? No, this was an emergency workaround from the very earliest days of the pandemic, before DOB's digital systems caught up to handle these transaction types online. Virtually all the transaction types listed here (new job filings, permit renewals, Letters of No Objection, etc.) are now processed through DOB's current digital filing systems rather than requiring physical mail or drop-off. Are DOB permit expiration extensions like the ones described here still active? No, those extensions were tied specifically to the Mayoral Emergency Executive Order No. 107 and the COVID-19 state of emergency, both of which ended years ago. Current permits follow standard expiration and renewal timelines with no active extension in place. Is there still a process for requesting "Essential Construction" approval to work during a shutdown? No, that specific request type was created only for the March-April 2020 construction shutdown period and isn't a standing or recurring DOB process today. Where can I find NYC DOB's current, non-emergency filing and permitting procedures instead of this 2020 guidance? NYC DOB's standard digital systems, eFiling and DOB NOW: Build, now handle the vast majority of permit, filing, and renewal transactions described in this post, without the borough-office mail/drop-off workaround that was specific to the initial pandemic response.
