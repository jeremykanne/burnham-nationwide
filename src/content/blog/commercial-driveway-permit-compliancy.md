## <strong>Commercial Driveway Permits ....</strong>  
<em>Keep in Compliance with Help from The Experts at Burnham Nationwide.</em>

In April 2021, the Chicago Department of Transportation (CDOT) discovered that there were over 19,000 commercial driveway permits with unpaid fees. This was in addition to an undetermined number of existing driveways that lacked driveway permits entirely. Subsequently, the Office of Inspector (OIG) recommended corrective actions that included having the Department of Business Affairs and Consumer Protection (BACP) and CDOT integrate their permit databases, among other measures, to ensure the proper enforcement of permit fees and fines.

In an OIG report originally published in 2019<sup>1</sup>, it was noted that effective November 2021, the BACP will begin the process of placing holds on existing business licenses and existing permits for driveways deemed non-compliant, i.e., a driveway that doesn’t have a permit but is required, or a driveway permit with outstanding fees.

To avoid any disruption, businesses/owners with an associated non-compliant driveway must initiative efforts to either, acquire the needed driveway permit, or remit any outstanding payments due. Only after which will applicable business license(s) and other permits will be eligible for renewal.

What happens if my business has an unpaid or missing commercial driveway permit? Since BACP began enforcement in November 2021, the city can place holds on your business license renewal and other permits until the driveway issue is resolved - either by paying outstanding fees or applying for the missing permit. This isn't a one-time cleanup; it's an ongoing, integrated check between CDOT and BACP databases. How much does a commercial driveway permit cost annually? Per CDOT's current fee schedule, commercial driveways up to 25 feet wide run $100 per year, and those between 25–50 feet wide run $120 per year - fees scale up further for wider driveways, so confirm your specific width-based tier. What documents do I need to apply for a new commercial driveway permit? You'll need a completed application, a dimensioned site plan, photographs of the proposed driveway (including sidewalk and curb detail), proof of property ownership (deed, FEIN or SSN, and the property's PIN), a Certificate of Insurance with endorsement page, and an Alley Access Ordinance if the driveway connects to an alley. How long does it take to get a new commercial driveway permit approved? Plan for up to six months from submission of a complete application to permit issuance - this is a longstanding timeline for this specific permit type, so it's worth starting the process well before you need the driveway operational. Why did Chicago start cracking down on unpermitted and delinquent driveways? A 2021 CDOT review uncovered more than 19,000 commercial driveway permits with unpaid fees, plus an unknown number of driveways with no permit at all. An Inspector General audit pushed CDOT and BACP to integrate their databases specifically to close that enforcement gap and recover the lost fee revenue.
