DOB NOW launched in Summer 2019 as New York City’s new Department of Buildings self-service online tool, created to increase efficiency and expedite the permit process. At the end of 2020, DOB NOW: Build will be expanding and receiving upgrades and Burnham is here to share changes and dates to look out for.

## New DOB NOW: Build Work Types

-   <strong>General Construction (GC)</strong> includes: Construction, Chimney, Façade Enlargement
-   <strong>Foundation (FO)</strong> includes: Deep, Retaining Wall, Shallow, Tie Backs and Anchors, Underpinning
-   <strong>Earthwork (EA)</strong> includes: Excavation, Landscape, Sitework (Grading and fill), Soil Improvement, Soil Improvements, Underpinning
-   <strong>Support of Excavation (SE)</strong> includes: Berming/Sloping/Benching, Ground Freezing, Shoring/Bracing, Slurry Shaft/Wall, Soil Grouting/Improvement/Mixing, Tangent/Secant Piles, Tie backs and Anchors
-   <strong>Protection & Mechanical Methods (PM)</strong> includes: Chute, Cocoon, Hoisting Equipment, Mechanical Demolition Equipment, Platform, Roof Overhead Protection, Roof Protection

### New DOB NOW: Build Additions

-   Composite permits
-   Site Safety Plan
-   Tenant Protection Plan
-   Supersede & Withdrawal of Applicant of Record or Contractor/Licensee
-   New features (improved search, exporting jobs, etc.)

## DOB NOW Training

-   <em>FIlings in DOB NOW:</em> Intended for Design professionals and Filing representatives<em>.</em> NYC DOB will host [six online training sessions](https://www1.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/dob-now-training.page) between December 10 - December 30.The sessions will cover the new work types and improved features.
-   <em>Permits in DOB NOW:</em> Intended for Contractors & Licensees. NYC DOB will host [six online training sessions](https://www1.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/dob-now-training.page) between December 11 - December 30. The sessions will cover requesting and renewing permits, new contractor requirements, contractor withdrawal and improved features.
-   <em>All Audiences requiring Tenant Protection Plan or Site Safety Plan:</em> NYC DOB will host [two online training sessions](https://www1.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/dob-now-training.page) on December 23 and January 7, 2021.Note: the ‘Filings in DOB NOW’ course should be taken prior to taking this course. The sessions will cover Tenant Protection Plans and Site Safety Plans.
-   <em>Supersede & Withdrawal in DOB NOW:</em> NYC DOB will host [two online training sessions](https://www1.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/dob-now-training.page) on January 5 and January 7, 2021. Note: the ‘Filings in DOB NOW’ course should be taken prior to taking this course. The sessions will show users how to use the superseding and withdrawal functions.

The free training sessions that the Department of Buildings are offering are intended to help industry members and the general public to learn more about DOB NOW. If you have any questions or need assistance through the process, please contact our team! We’re in this together.

Do I still need to take a training course to use DOB NOW: Build today? No specific scheduled course like the ones in this post - but DOB NOW now requires an NYC.ID account to log in (a requirement added in June 2024), separate from any eFiling credentials you may have used previously. What's the biggest recent change for co-op or condo renovation filings in DOB NOW? As of January 26, 2026, the condo or co-op board is automatically added as a stakeholder on job filings when the owner type is a unit owner or shareholder, and the board must formally attest in DOB NOW that it authorized the filing before DOB will process the permit - filing in parallel with pending board approval is no longer an option. Has anything changed recently about sidewalk shed permits in DOB NOW? Yes - as of February 2, 2026, all sidewalk shed permits expire every 90 days and no longer auto-renew, under Local Laws 48 and 51 of 2025, specifically to prevent sheds from lingering on streets long after construction has wrapped up. Renewal now requires showing active progress on the underlying work. Are there new restrictions on filing structural permits separately from a related new building or alteration job? Yes - starting February 2, 2026, DOB NOW restricts issuing or renewing a Structural, Foundation, or Earthwork permit filed as a separate Alteration when it's actually connected to a development, enlargement, or change of use, closing a previous workaround that let some structural work bypass fuller review. Is there a new electrical code coming to DOB NOW soon? Yes - beginning June 29, 2026, DOB NOW: Build electrical filings must comply with the 2025 NYC Electrical Code, and electrical inspection requests are moving to a new "DOB NOW: Inspections Plus" module rather than the current Inspections system.
