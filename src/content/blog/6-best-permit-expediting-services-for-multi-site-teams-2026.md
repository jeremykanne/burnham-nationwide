## How do you evaluate a permit expediter for multi-site commercial work?

Start by asking how many jurisdictions the firm has worked in and whether they have managed simultaneous permit programs for national brands. A provider that has pulled permits in 50 cities is operating at a different level than one that has worked in 5.

Request specific examples of multi-site engagements, including the number of locations, jurisdictions involved, and how the firm handled varying local requirements.

According to [research from Princeton University and the University of Chicago](https://evansoltas.com/papers/Durations_DSW2026.pdf), permitting duration varies significantly across U.S. cities. In the fastest cities, entire projects are completed in the time it takes the slowest cities to issue approvals alone. For multi-site teams, this variability means your expediting partner needs real experience in each specific jurisdiction where you build.

Ask about the project management structure. Will you have a dedicated project manager, or will your account be shared among multiple coordinators? For multi-site programs, a single point of contact who understands your project scope and schedule constraints is critical.

Evaluate whether the firm can identify code compliance issues before submission. Providers with in-house [code consulting](/code-consulting-process/) capabilities, like Burnham Nationwide, catch zoning, accessibility, and life safety issues during due diligence rather than after the building department flags them. This early intervention can eliminate entire correction rounds and keep your schedule intact.

## What is the difference between permit expediting and permit management software?

Permit expediting is a professional service where a firm manages the entire permit process on your behalf. The expediter prepares applications, submits plans, coordinates with the building department, manages correction comments, and tracks permits through issuance. Your team delegates the process to a partner who has jurisdiction-specific knowledge and established relationships with municipal staff.

Permit management software, by contrast, gives your internal team tools to organize and track the permitting process. Platforms like PermitFlow automate research, form generation, and status tracking, but coordination with building departments and correction resolution remains with your staff.

For multi-site teams deciding between the two, the choice often depends on internal capacity. If you have experienced [permit coordinators](/what-we-do/permit-management/) on staff and need organizational tools, software can help. If you need someone to own the process end to end across dozens of jurisdictions, a full-service expediter like Burnham Nationwide is the more effective path.
