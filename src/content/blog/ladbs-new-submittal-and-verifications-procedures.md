Burnham's goal, as always, is to continue communicating with city officials online, via email and by phone and provide you with updates on any city implementation that may take place in the coming weeks. We will continue to monitor all jurisdictions for updates and will send out new processes as they’re announced.

In March 2020, LADBS established designated “Drop-off/Pickup” areas for new submittal and verification plans.To further improve this process, LADBS will now offer limited in-person appointments to facilitate quicker resolution and improve the efficiency of the plan check process. The following submittal and verification procedures have been implemented and are effective as of Monday, September 21, 2020:

-   **New submittals** \- when dropping off a new submittal, the staff member available will assign a “screening appointment” (paper print out) where someone will call you while they’re screening plans and application. It’s important to stay available at this scheduled time otherwise plans **will not** get screened! This is required for all new submittals.
-   **Back room plan checks** - resubmittals will only be accepted by appointment with the plan checker. Proof of this appointment will need to be provided at time of drop off.
-   **OTC verification** - will remain the same and packages must contain the following:

-   An LADBS “Drop-off” transmittal cover sheet, wrapped around the plans, with the project information clearly visible
-   Original marked plans
-   Revised plans
-   Original correction sheets with a written response addressing each pending correction
-   Any other required documents per the plan check engineer’s instruction.

We appreciate the hard work the DBS is continuously doing. Please [contact Burnham](/contact/los-angeles/) with any questions regarding your project. We will be here to answer any questions you may have.

Are LADBS plan submittals still handled through drop-off appointments like this post describes? No - that 2020 drop-off/screening-appointment system has been replaced. Today, most plan check submittals go through ePlanLA, LADBS's fully electronic plan review system, which handles everything from submission through verification and permit issuance digitally rather than requiring physical drop-off. What's required to use LADBS's current online submittal systems? You need to register for an Angeleno Account first — LADBS's single sign-on system that also provides access to other LA city department services, like the Bureau of Engineering and City Planning. Once registered, you can use ePlanLA for projects requiring plan review, or PermitLA for simpler projects that qualify for Express Permits without plan review. Do all LADBS permits require full electronic plan review now? No, LADBS still offers three distinct tracks: Counter Plan Check (same-day, in-person review for small/medium projects, taking 45-60 minutes), Expanded Counter Plan Check (2-3 hours, for medium projects like ADUs), and Regular Plan Check (electronic, 4-8 weeks, for large or complex commercial/multi-family projects). How long does a typical Regular Plan Check review take in Los Angeles today? Generally 4 to 8 weeks depending on current workload, submitted and reviewed entirely through ePlanLA - paying an additional 50% of the plan check fee can expedite that review timeline if needed. Do I still need to bring physical correction sheets and marked-up plans for resubmittals? For electronic Regular Plan Check submittals, no - that requirement was specific to the 2020-era in-person back-room plan check process described in this post. Current resubmittals for projects in ePlanLA are handled digitally through the same online system used for the original submission.
